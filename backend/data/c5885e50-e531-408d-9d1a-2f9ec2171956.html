五、文法口訣

六、考前小測驗

1. The report must be _____ by Friday.
(A) submit　(B) submitted　(C) submitting　(D) submission
答案：B。被動語態 must be + p.p.。
2. We look forward to _____ you at the seminar.
(A) meet　(B) meeting　(C) met　(D) meets
答案：B。look forward to 的 to 是介系詞，後面接 V-ing。
3. The manager is responsible _____ training new employees.
(A) of　(B) to　(C) for　(D) with
答案：C。固定搭配 be responsible for。
